n0passtemps registration ceremony

Enrols a WebAuthn authenticator against a subject, using the two round trips of POST /v1/webauthn/{subject_ref}/register and POST /v1/webauthn/{subject_ref}/register/complete.

Development only. This page sends the API key from the browser. In a deployment the key belongs on your server: the front end calls your backend, and your backend calls this service. Do not paste a production key here, and do not copy this fetch pattern into an application.
